# Asset credits

Everything in this folder is released under [CC0 1.0](CC0-1.0.txt) (public domain).
No credit is required, but here is where it came from.

## Sound effects (`sfx/`)

From [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) sound packs (Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio,
Digital Audio), CC0. Converted to MP3 from the copies in the
[`@netforge/asset-library-files`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@netforge/asset-library-files) npm package.

## Lighting (`hdri/`)

Photographed environment maps from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/hdris), CC0, as resized by
[`@pmndrs/assets`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@pmndrs/assets): `lobby.exr` and `park.exr`.

## 3D models (`models/`)

Low-poly models by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), CC0: trees, bushes, rocks, flowers and stumps
from the Nature Kit, and buildings from the City Kit (Commercial). Optimized copies from the
[`@netforge/asset-library-files`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@netforge/asset-library-files) npm package.
